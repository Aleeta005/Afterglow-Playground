Multi-Touch & Controls

TOUCH AUDIO When sound is enabled, gentle notes play only when you touch or drag across the canvas.
HOLD / SWIPE Hold steady to focus energy; flick rapidly to unleash bursts of particles and harmonic chimes.
GENERATIVE AUDIO Every touch synthesizes real-time pentatonic ripples, warm synth pads, and celesta bells.
PAUSE / RESET Freeze a masterpiece mid-motion with Pause, or clear the canvas anytime with Reset.
